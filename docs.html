Your Engine

Your own agent in Telegram and on engine.oddsengine.live. It scans any coin, reads charts, watches your wallet and your bags, and briefs you every morning. Free scans, everything else is paid per answer from your balance.

What it does

🔎Scan any coinPaste a contract, a chart link or a $ticker. Bundles, clusters, dev, locks, token forwards and a live holder map.
📈Read the chartTrend, RSI, MACD, support and resistance from real candles. Facts, no guessing.
🛡️Wallet GuardAn alert in seconds when your wallet approves an unknown spender. Revoke in one tap.
⚠️Bag watchDev sells, wallet groups dumping, liquidity pulled, locks ending: on every coin you hold.
☀️Morning briefingWeather, calendar, news, stocks and your coins in one message, at your time.
💬Ask anythingEvery major AI model, images, voice notes, reminders, alerts, YouTube summaries.

Start in Telegram: open the bot, connect your wallet on engine.oddsengine.live once, and talk to it like a person. Your Engine never holds your keys. Anything that moves funds is signed by your own wallet.

Scans

Send a contract address, a mint, a chart link or just $TICKER. Scans are free.

  • Chains: Solana, Robinhood Chain, Base, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Arbitrum.
  • Tickers: searched across the whole market. Established coins first, then the coin with real volume and real trades. Pools that fake liquidity are ignored. If two real coins share a ticker, Your Engine asks you for the contract instead of guessing.
  • The card: verdict, market, safety, holders, dev, locks with unlock date, copycats and narrative. It arrives in seconds.
  • Deep scan: about a minute later the bundles, snipers, fresh wallets, same funder clusters and token forwards are in. It lands in your chat on Telegram and on the website, and the full report updates by itself.
  • Token forwards: tokens sent from wallet to wallet without a sale. Early buyers or the dev hiding supply in new wallets shows up here, with what those wallets still hold and whether the same collection wallets did this on other coins.
  • Full report: chart, live trades, the 3D holder map and project research. Opens right inside Telegram, no login needed.

Charts and X

Ask "is SOL bullish?" or "TA on $PONS". Your Engine pulls real candles (Coinbase, GeckoTerminal, Yahoo for stocks) and computes EMA 20/50/200, RSI, MACD, volatility, support, resistance and volume on 1h and 1d. It tells you what the chart shows, never where the price goes.

Ask "what is X saying about $TORCH" for a live read of posts on X: how many it read, the mood split, the loud accounts and signs of botted shilling, with links. When it finds little, it says so.

Wallet Guard

Most wallet drains start with one bad approval from a scam link. Turn Wallet Guard on (say "turn on wallet guard" or use the switch on the site) and Your Engine watches your connected wallets.

🚨 Dangerous approval on 0x7a3f…c21e
Spender 0x9f2c…ef01: a plain wallet, not a contract.
It can now move unlimited TORCH. Revoke it now, before anything is taken.
  • Approvals: token approvals, NFT approve all and Permit2 to a spender we do not know. Known routers like Uniswap, 1inch or Torch do not alert you.
  • Solana: new delegates and changes of token account owner.
  • Tokens taken: when someone moves your tokens with an old approval.
  • Tokens leaving without a swap: if it was not you, your key may be exposed. Move the rest to a fresh wallet.
  • Revoke now: every alert has a button. Your own wallet signs one small transaction that sets the permission to zero.
  • Wallet check: say "check my wallet" for every open approval, with risk and a revoke button, plus one tap to revoke all risky ones.
Good to know. The chain records which contract got an approval, not the website you signed it on. Connected sites live in your wallet app (MetaMask: Connected sites, Phantom: Connected apps, Rabby: Connected dapps). Disconnecting a site does not remove an approval. Revoking does. If your seed phrase or private key is exposed, nothing can stop a drain: move everything to a new wallet. Never type a seed phrase anywhere, Your Engine will never ask for it.

Live alerts: Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum and Solana. The wallet check covers Base, Ethereum and BNB Chain too.

Bag watch

Part of Wallet Guard. Every scanner checks a coin once, before you buy. Your Engine keeps watching every coin you hold and messages you when:

  • the dev sells, with how much of their bag and of the supply,
  • a top holder or a wallet group with the same funder dumps,
  • liquidity is pulled,
  • the price drops 30% within minutes,
  • a token lock is about to end or has ended.
⚠️ $TEST: something just happened in a coin you hold
🧑‍💻 Dev sold 50% of their bag (5% of supply), still holds 5%.
💧 Liquidity fell from $50K to $20K. Possible LP pull.

Coins in your wallets are picked up by themselves (worth $5 or more). Add any other coin with "watch $XYZ", stop one with "stop watching $XYZ".

OddsEngine AI

Every major AI model in one chat and behind one API key. You pay per message with $ODDS. No subscription, no card, no email.

Start in three steps

  1. Connect a wallet. MetaMask, Rabby or any wallet that works on Robinhood Chain. You sign one message. That is not a transaction and costs nothing.
  2. Top up. Pick an amount in dollars, send the $ODDS your wallet shows you. Your balance is in dollars from then on.
  3. Chat. Pick a model at the top of the chat and start typing. Switch the model whenever you like, even inside the same conversation.

The chat

  • Your chats are saved to your wallet. Open them on any device after you connect the same wallet.
  • Attach up to 4 images per message to any model marked sees images. You can also paste them.
  • Pick a model under Create images and describe what you want. Each image has a download button.
  • Under every answer you see what it cost.
  • OddsEngine auto picks a strong model for each question. You pay what the picked model costs.
  • Compare: tap Compare at the top, pick 2 or 3 models, and your next message goes to all of them at once. The answers show up side by side with what each one cost. Pick one to continue the chat with it.
  • Free to try: models marked Free cost nothing. You can send 20 free messages a day after you connect a wallet, no top up needed.

Prices and fees

You pay what the model maker charges plus our fee. The fee is already inside every price on the site, nothing is added later.

Holdings are checked when you send a message and remembered for a few minutes. Tokens in linked Torch wallets count. Your current fee is shown on the Account page.

Before a message runs we hold back the most it could cost, then charge what it really cost. You can never go below zero, and two messages at once can never spend the same dollar twice.

Top up

With a fixed amount (recommended)

Choose $5, $10, $25, $50 or your own amount. We hold the $ODDS price for 10 minutes. Send the exact amount shown and you get exactly that many dollars, even if the price moves while your transfer is on its way.

Any amount

Send any amount of $ODDS on Robinhood Chain to the deposit address on the Account page. It is credited at the live price within about a minute.

Send from the wallet you connected or a wallet you linked. That is how we know the transfer is yours. A transfer from an unknown wallet is not lost, it waits and is credited as soon as you connect or link that wallet.

Your balance is kept in dollars. If $ODDS goes up or down afterwards, your balance stays the same.

Holding on Torch

A Torch wallet can not sign in on other websites, so you connect a normal wallet first. Then link your Torch wallet once:

  1. On the Account page, enter your Torch wallet address.
  2. Send 1 $ODDS from your Torch wallet to your own connected wallet. The token stays yours, it only proves both wallets belong to you.
  3. Done. Your Torch holdings count for the fee, and you can top up straight from Torch.

Your $ODDS stay in your Torch wallet, so you keep earning Torch holder rewards.

API quickstart

The API works like the OpenAI API. Create a key on the Account page, then use any OpenAI client.

curl https://api.oddsengine.live/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer oe_live_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model":"anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}'
from openai import OpenAI
client = OpenAI(base_url="https://api.oddsengine.live/v1", api_key="oe_live_...")
r = client.chat.completions.create(model="openai/gpt-6.1-sol",
    messages=[{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}])
print(r.choices[0].message.content)
  • GET /v1/models lists every model with our prices.
  • POST /v1/chat/completions supports stream: true, tools and JSON output where the model supports them.
  • Each response has usage.oddsengine_charged_usd, the exact amount taken from your balance.
  • Use oddsengine/auto as the model to let us pick.
  • Keys are stored as a hash. A new key is shown once. Revoke a key any time on the Account page.

For agents: pay per request with x402

Your agent needs no account and no API key. It needs a wallet with money in it. It calls the API, gets 402 Payment Required with a price, signs a payment and sends the request again. We settle it on chain and answer. The agent pays no gas.

Endpoint: POST https://api.oddsengine.live/v1/chat/completions, same body as OpenAI. 460+ models, pick one with "model".

Pay in USDC on Base (any x402 client)

We speak standard x402 (v1 and v2, scheme exact, USDC on Base). Any x402 library works without changes. Our margin from every payment buys $ODDS, half of it gets burned. See every buyback and burn.

JavaScript

npm i @x402/fetch @x402/evm viem

import { wrapFetchWithPaymentFromConfig } from "@x402/fetch";
import { ExactEvmScheme } from "@x402/evm";
import { privateKeyToAccount } from "viem/accounts";

const account = privateKeyToAccount(process.env.AGENT_KEY);
const pay = wrapFetchWithPaymentFromConfig(fetch, {
  schemes: [{ network: "eip155:8453", client: new ExactEvmScheme(account) }],
});
const r = await pay("https://api.oddsengine.live/v1/chat/completions", {
  method: "POST", headers: { "Content-Type": "application/json" },
  body: JSON.stringify({ model: "deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash",
    messages: [{ role: "user", content: "Hello" }] }),
});
console.log((await r.json()).choices[0].message.content);

Python

pip install "x402[requests,evm]" eth-account

from eth_account import Account
from x402 import x402ClientSync
from x402.mechanisms.evm import EthAccountSigner
from x402.mechanisms.evm.exact import ExactEvmScheme
from x402.http.clients import x402_requests

client = x402ClientSync()
client.register("eip155:8453", ExactEvmScheme(signer=EthAccountSigner(Account.from_key(AGENT_KEY))))
s = x402_requests(client)
r = s.post("https://api.oddsengine.live/v1/chat/completions",
           json={"model": "deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash", "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "Hello"}]})
print(r.json()["choices"][0]["message"]["content"])

Pay in $ODDS on Robinhood Chain

Pick the permit option of the 402 answer. Sign an EIP-2612 permit for that amount and send the request again with the X-PAYMENT header. Get $ODDS on Torch first.

X-PAYMENT: base64({"x402Version":1,"scheme":"permit","network":"robinhood",
  "payload":{"owner":"0x...","value":"<maxAmountRequired>","deadline":1790000000,"signature":"0x..."}})
  • Spender, token, chain id and the EIP-712 domain are in extra of the permit option.

Good to know

  • One payment covers at least $0.05 plus $0.01 for gas. What the request does not use stays as credit on the paying wallet.
  • Next time, if the credit covers the request, we use the credit and move no money. The agent still signs, that proves it is the same wallet.
  • The answer carries PAYMENT-RESPONSE (and X-PAYMENT-RESPONSE) with the transaction.
  • Use a normal wallet (EOA) for the agent and keep only small amounts on it.

Agent keys and MCP

A key for your agent, without a browser

Your agent signs in with its own wallet and gets a key with limits you choose.

GET  /v1/engine/nonce?address=0xYOUR_WALLET     -> { "message": "..." }
sign the message with personal_sign
POST /v1/agents/key
{"address":"0x...","signature":"0x...","name":"my bot","day_cap":5,"models":["anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5"]}
  • day_cap is the most this key may spend per day, in dollars. Leave it out for no limit.
  • models limits the key to these model ids. Leave it out for all models.
  • Change limits later with POST /v1/ai/keys/limits {"id","day_cap","models"}.

Errors and limits

CodeMeaning
400Something in the request is wrong, the message says what.
401Missing or wrong API key.
402Not enough balance for this request. Top up or pick a cheaper model.
404Unknown model ID. See /v1/models.
429Too many requests. The limit is 60 per minute per key. Free models also have a limit of 20 messages a day per wallet.
502 / 503The model maker had a problem. Try again or use another model. Failed requests are not charged.

Your data

  • Chats on the site are saved so you can come back to them. Messages, titles and images are encrypted in our database.
  • Images in chats are deleted after 30 days. Download the ones you want to keep.
  • Delete one chat or all chats at any time. Deleted means gone.
  • API requests: we keep the model, token counts and cost for your usage list. We do not store the text of API requests.
  • What you send goes to the maker of the model you picked so it can answer.
