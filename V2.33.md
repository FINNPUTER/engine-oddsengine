# OddsEngine 2.33: Your Engine

## What is new
- Your Engine: a personal agent on engine.oddsengine.live and in Telegram (text and voice notes).
- Reads Robinhood Chain directly: wallet holdings, recent buys and sells, ODDS and TORCH burned, latest Torch launches, token info and prices.
- Also: engine calls and record, crypto markets, Polymarket search, web search, read a link, watch a page for changes.
- Reminders, price alerts, watched pages and a morning briefing at the user's own hour. The server sends them on its own, the user never has to write first.
- Picks its own model for thinking and tools: fast model for normal questions, deep model for analysis. Override with MOTOR_FAST / MOTOR_DEEP.
- Every model of OddsEngine AI inside Your Engine: "make me an image of ..." creates the picture (also by voice note in Telegram, the image arrives as a photo), "ask GPT ..." passes the question to that model. Default models for answers and images per user, set on the page or by saying "use Grok from now on". Standard image model: MOTOR_IMAGE (google/gemini-3.1-flash-image).
- Images and model answers are charged like in the chat: real cost plus the OddsEngine fee, from the same balance.
- Access: wallet, linked Telegram and a $ODDS balance (MOTOR_MIN_CREDIT, default $0.05). Without Telegram the chat stays locked.
- Billing: everything comes off the balance. Answers, images and other models at real cost plus the OddsEngine fee. Briefing, reminders, alerts and watched pages have no AI cost and run while there is a balance. Empty balance: one message, then everything pauses until the next top up.
- Fix: web search calls are now charged at their real cost (before, the reserve cap could charge less than the cost).

- Free trial: every Telegram account gets MOTOR_TRIAL free messages (10) without balance. Counted per Telegram account, not per wallet. Only the chain, market and calls tools, no images, web search, other models, reminders or trips. All trials together cost at most MOTOR_TRIAL_DAY_USD per day (3 $), paid by OddsEngine.
- Weather and trips: "I live in ...", "I drive to the gym every weekday at 5". Weather worldwide from MET Norway (free). Before each saved trip it checks live traffic (TomTom, TOMTOM_API_KEY on web, the same key as HENRY) and messages the user. Weather and trips also in the morning briefing. Each traffic check costs MOTOR_ROUTE_USD (0.002 $) from the balance. Variables: MOTOR_ROUTE_LEAD_MIN (45), MOTOR_MAX_ROUTES (6).

- Torch holder rewards: the user tells Your Engine their public Torch profile name once, then it says what they can claim (ETH or TORCH, per coin, in USD), also in the briefing.
- News: follow public Telegram channels (Watcher Guru, Whale Alert, Cointelegraph, any public channel with web preview) or RSS feeds (Federal Reserve, SEC, any RSS link), optionally only posts with certain words. New posts come as Telegram messages right away or go into the briefing. At most MOTOR_NEWS_MAX_DAY (30) messages per day, no AI cost, runs while there is a balance.
- Burn page: new endpoint GET /v1/burns/torch with Torch's own buyback numbers for $ODDS and $TORCH (5 minute cache).

- Torch market: new coins, trending, top, graduated (most recent first, from the moment OddsEngine saw it) and close to graduation with progress, from Torch's own overview.

- Intelligence: the user picks how Your Engine thinks, Auto (Haiku, Sonnet for analysis), Fast (Haiku), Smart (Sonnet) or Max (Opus, MOTOR_MAX), with the estimated cost per answer shown on the page.
- Name: it greets new users with their Telegram first name, asks once how they want to be called and keeps it in memory (visible and deletable on the page).

- Beta testers in admin.html: paste wallets, give each a one-time start credit (max 10 $, paid by OddsEngine), see balance, usage and Telegram status, remove testers. Works without redeploy, ENGINE_BETA still works too.

- Support inbox: every message to the bot, /support <text> in Telegram, feedback to Your Engine and the Support form on engine.oddsengine.live are saved as tickets (table support_tickets). admin.html shows them (open, answered, done), reply goes to the person's Telegram. Replying in Telegram to a relayed message marks the ticket answered. Screenshots: photos sent to the bot and images in the website form are stored with the ticket. AI sorting: each new ticket gets area, category, severity, title and summary (SUPPORT_AI_MODEL, Gemini Flash Lite, reads screenshots too). "Bundle open tickets" in admin.html groups all open tickets into work items ordered by urgency (SUPPORT_REPORT_MODEL, Claude Sonnet). Paid by OddsEngine, fractions of a cent per ticket. Off: SUPPORT_AI=off.

## Endpoints
POST /v1/engine/ask, GET /v1/engine/agent, POST /v1/engine/agent/models, POST /v1/engine/agent/remove, /v1/engine/agent/briefing, /v1/engine/agent/briefing/now, /v1/engine/agent/clear

## Upload
1. This ZIP into the platform repo (web and worker deploy).
2. Variable on web for a private test: ENGINE_BETA = comma list of wallets that get Your Engine while the switch is off.
3. Engine site ZIP v18 into the engine repo.
4. Feedback: users write "feedback: ..." to Your Engine, it goes privately to the operator chat (AI_ALERT_CHAT_ID or TELEGRAM_PRIVATE_CHAT_ID) with the last messages. Optional MOTOR_FEEDBACK_URL on web: invite link of a private tester group, shown under the chat.
4b. Beta label: Your Engine shows "Beta" on the page until MOTOR_BETA_LABEL=off is set on web.
5. After the test: switch "Engine page: Your Engine tab" on in admin.html.
