# OddsEngine 2.34: Kalshi Edition

Contains everything from 2.33 (Your Engine, support inbox). If 2.33 is not uploaded yet, upload this one instead.

## What is new
- The engine runs on every Kalshi weather market: daily high and daily low, all US cities Kalshi lists (about 20 cities, 37 markets per day right now), at Kalshi's own stations (Central Park, Midway and so on, read from the rule text "(CLIxxx)").
- Same model and same gates as on Polymarket: ensemble at the station, measured values of the day folded in (a high can only rise, a low only fall), block choice with engine.best_block, honest number price + k x (model minus price).
- Prices include the Kalshi fee (0.07 x P x (1 minus P) per contract, KALSHI_FEE).
- Station correction from day one: the forecast of the day before (Open-Meteo previous runs, same models) against Kalshi's settled result for the last 30 days, per city and high/low. After 15 own scored days the engine uses its own numbers.
- First call per market is frozen and scored against Kalshi's result. No automatic posting (KALSHI_POST=on turns Telegram posts on).
- In the weather app, tab Kalshi on public.oddsengine.live (app 1.9.11): open calls, every Kalshi market with engine vs Kalshi price per field, record. public.oddsengine.live/kalshi opens that tab. Your Engine answers Kalshi questions (tool kalshi_calls).
- Switch "Kalshi" in admin.html: off = runs and scores in the background, only you see it (admin.html section and the page with your admin key). On = public.

2. App ZIP 1.9.11 into the app repo.
