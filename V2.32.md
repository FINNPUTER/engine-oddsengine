# OddsEngine 2.32: x402 in USDC, buyback and burn of $ODDS

## What is new
- Agents pay with standard x402 in USDC on Base (scheme exact, EIP-3009). x402 v1 (X-PAYMENT) and v2 (PAYMENT-SIGNATURE / PAYMENT-REQUIRED / PAYMENT-RESPONSE) both work. Any x402 library works without custom code.
- Our server settles the USDC itself with the gas wallet on Base. No Coinbase account, no outside facilitator.
- Margin from every USDC payment goes into a buyback pot. From $5 (BUYBACK_MIN_USD) the server buys $ODDS on Robinhood Chain (Across, fallback Relay), sends 50% to 0x...dEaD (burn) and 50% to the revenue wallet.
- Everything else (model cost, agent credit) is swept as USDC to USDC_SWEEP_TO (default: AI_DEPOSIT_ADDRESS, your revenue wallet, same address on Base).
- Public log: GET /v1/x402/buybacks
- Admin: POST /v1/admin/buyback {"usd": 2} buys back now with extra USDC on the treasury (button on admin.html).
- New switch on admin.html: "x402 in USDC (Base)". Off by default.

## Upload
1. This ZIP into the platform repo (web and worker deploy).
2. Optional variables on web: X402_USDC_DAY_USD (100), BUYBACK_MIN_USD (5), BUYBACK_BURN_PCT (50), USDC_SWEEP_TO, BASE_RPC, X402_BASE_MIN_ETH (0.0003).
3. The gas wallet needs a little ETH on Base (done).
4. Turn on the switch "x402 in USDC (Base)".

## Safety
- Daily limit in dollars, Telegram alert when hit.
- Each signature only once (our record and the nonce on chain).
- Gas wallet on Base only holds the open buyback pot, the rest is swept.
- Alerts: low ETH on Base, bridge without arrival after 1 hour, burn stuck.

## 2.32.1
