Deploy the OddsEngine Locker

Your wallet deploys the contract and becomes its admin. The admin can only change the fee for new locks (capped in the contract) and where ETH fees go. Nobody can touch locked tokens.

Costs only a little ETH for gas. After the deploy: copy the address into your admin page (section Locker), press Save address, then turn on the switch Locker.
